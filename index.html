Assalamu Alaikum, I’m Fahad Bin Aman, a student and explorer from Cumilla, Bangladesh.

I’m passionate about photography, nature, travel, science, space, books, technology, and Islamic history.

Through photography, I enjoy capturing landscapes, everyday moments, places, architecture, and the beauty of the world around me.

This website is a personal space where I can share my photographs, journeys, interests, and creative work.
